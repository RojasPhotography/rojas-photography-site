## TOP 3 RECOMMENDATIONS

### Recommendation 1: Add Service Definitions
**Problem:** No FAQ answers "What IS professional corporate photography?"
**Solution:** Add 1-2 sentence definition section above each service page
**Expected Impact:** 20-30% traffic lift for "what is" queries
**Effort:** 15 minutes per page × 5 = 1.25 hours

Example:
```
"Professional in-studio headshots are guided photography sessions that produce
polished, authentic portraits for LinkedIn, company websites, and business marketing.
Unlike casual photos, professional headshots use proper lighting, posing direction,
and professional editing."
```

### Recommendation 2: Embed Quotable Facts in FAQ Answers
**Problem:** Prices and stats in metadata, not in FAQ body text
**Solution:** Rewrite FAQ answers to include specific numbers inline
**Expected Impact:** 40-50% increase in AI citations
**Effort:** 30 minutes per service page × 5 = 2.5 hours

Example improvement:
```
BEFORE: "Sessions are $150, plus $150 per image."
AFTER: "Rojas Photography charges $150 for the session fee, plus $150 per
image selected. Most clients select 3-5 images for a total of $600–$1,150.
All images are delivered within 48 hours."
```

### Recommendation 3: Add Comparison Tables
**Problem:** No side-by-side comparisons (Studio vs. On-Site, etc.)
**Solution:** Create 3-column comparison tables for each service
**Expected Impact:** 10-15% conversion lift from clarity
**Effort:** 45 minutes per table × 3 = 2.25 hours

Example:
```
| Feature | Studio Headshots | On-Site Photography |
|---------|------------------|---------------------|
| Cost | $300–$450 person | $150–$300 person |
| Travel | 30 min to studio | None (we come) |
| Duration | 30–45 min | 4–8 hours |
| Best for | 1–5 people | 10+ people |
| Turnaround | 48 hours | 24 hours |
```
