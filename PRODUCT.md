# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Two distinct customers, and every page must signal which lane it serves within seconds. Primary: individual professionals in Modesto and the Central Valley — executives, attorneys, physicians, realtors, personal brands — who feel their current photo undersells the reputation they've built; they book the premium Signature studio experience. Secondary: companies and their HR/marketing leads (the Kaiser / Comcast / Save Mart tier) booking on-location team headshot days, event coverage, or recurring annual headshot programs. Context: they arrive from local Google search or referral, are often camera-shy, and decide within seconds whether this is "for me" and whether it looks worth premium pricing.

## Product Purpose

The marketing site for Rojas Photography LLC (rojasphotography.net), a husband-and-wife premium corporate photography and video studio in Modesto, CA. The site's job is to convert individuals into self-serve studio bookings (17hats scheduler at `/premium-headshots#booking-heading`) and companies into team-day or discovery-call conversations, while ranking for "[service] + Modesto / Central Valley" searches. Success is booked sessions and team-day inquiries, not traffic.

## Positioning

THE name for premium corporate photography in Modesto and the Central Valley — the studio where two photographers, Alfonso and Niomi, shoot every session, in-studio or brought to your office. Priority order behind every decision: brand first, then revenue and profit, then volume.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: Book a Studio Session (self-serve scheduler). Secondary: Headshot Day for Your Team; a free discovery call is the entry point for custom and larger projects only.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: **"The headshot your reputation deserves."**
- Belief ladder: (1) these photos are clearly better than what I have → (2) serious companies trust them — Kaiser, Comcast, Save Mart, California Lawyers Association, Tenet Health → (3) booking is easy and delivery is fast (same-day for on-site teams and booths, 48 hours for studio) → book.
- Proof on hand: the client names above; 500+ executives photographed; 60+ five-star Google reviews; named testimonials (Catherine Luke, Joyce Ulrich, Courtnay Lynch) already in the code; a live Google-reviews component ships once its API env vars are set.

## Brand Personality

Confident, restrained, premium. First-person plural always — "we," Alfonso and Niomi; never a solo "I." Warm in person, understated on the page; never salesy, never repeats its own catchphrases ("story," "authentic") within a page. StoryBrand stance throughout: the customer is the hero, we are the guide.

## Anti-references

- Anything that reads as discounted or volume-cheap. The team/Express lane must feel like a *different experience* (fast, self-serve, event-based), never a cheaper version of the Signature studio work.
- Prices in page titles or meta descriptions. Outcome and craft lead; transparent pricing appears on-page, lower down.
- Ornate, couture, fashion-house luxury aesthetics. The taste is modern, photographic, restrained — big imagery on quiet ground, not filigree.
- The old homepage's failure mode: six equal service cards competing at once, burying the two lanes that matter.

## Design Principles

1. The customer is the hero; we are the guide — StoryBrand SB7 shapes every page's narrative order.
2. Two lanes, one brand: every surface answers "individual or team?" in seconds without cheapening either.
3. The photography carries the design. Big, real client imagery; the design frames it quietly.
4. Premium is protected: restraint over hype, and price never leads.
5. Ready buyers never wait: a booking path within two clicks from anywhere; conversations are reserved for custom work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA as the working bar: AA color contrast on all text, descriptive alt text on every image (doubles as local SEO), `prefers-reduced-motion` honored for all reveal and entrance animations, visible focus outlines site-wide.
