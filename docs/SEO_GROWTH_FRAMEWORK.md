## 📚 SEO BEST PRACTICES GUIDE

### **On-Page SEO Checklist**

Use this for every page you create:

- [ ] **Title Tag**: 50-60 characters, includes target keyword
- [ ] **Meta Description**: 150-160 characters, includes keyword + CTA
- [ ] **H1 Tag**: One per page, includes primary keyword
- [ ] **H2 Tags**: Include secondary keywords, location terms
- [ ] **URL Structure**: Clean, includes keyword (e.g., /headshots-stockton)
- [ ] **Image Alt Tags**: Descriptive, includes keywords where natural
- [ ] **Internal Links**: Link to 3+ related pages
- [ ] **Schema Markup**: LocalBusiness, Service, or FAQPage
- [ ] **Mobile Friendly**: Test on mobile device
- [ ] **Page Speed**: Load time under 3 seconds
- [ ] **Call-to-Action**: Clear CTA above the fold

---

### **Content Quality Standards**

Every page should have:

- [ ] **Minimum 800 words** (1,200+ for competitive keywords)
- [ ] **5+ FAQ questions** (target featured snippets)
- [ ] **3-5 high-quality images** (optimized with Next.js Image)
- [ ] **1-2 testimonials** (social proof)
- [ ] **Clear contact info** (phone, email, booking link)
- [ ] **Natural keyword usage** (don't stuff keywords)
- [ ] **Unique content** (never duplicate from other pages)
- [ ] **Updated regularly** (refresh every 3-6 months)

---

### **Link Building Strategy**

**Internal Links**:
- Link from homepage to all service pages
- Cross-link between related services
- Blog posts link to relevant service pages
- Use descriptive anchor text

**External Links (Backlinks)**:
- [ ] Chamber of Commerce membership
- [ ] Local business association listings
- [ ] Guest posts on local blogs
- [ ] Local news features/press releases
- [ ] Client website testimonials (with backlink)
- [ ] Partnership pages
- [ ] Industry directory listings

**Monthly Backlink Goals**:
- Month 1-3: 5+ backlinks
- Month 4-6: 10+ backlinks
- Month 7-12: 20+ backlinks

## 🚨 COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

### **Don't Do These:**

❌ **Keyword Stuffing**: Using keywords unnaturally 20 times per page
✅ **Do This**: Use keywords naturally 3-5 times, focus on synonyms/related terms

❌ **Duplicate Content**: Copying content from other pages/sites
✅ **Do This**: Write unique content for every page

❌ **Ignoring Mobile**: Only optimizing for desktop
✅ **Do This**: Mobile-first design (60%+ traffic is mobile)

❌ **Buying Links**: Paying for backlinks from spammy sites
✅ **Do This**: Earn links through quality content and partnerships

❌ **Neglecting Reviews**: Ignoring Google Business reviews
✅ **Do This**: Actively request and respond to all reviews

❌ **Set It and Forget It**: Never updating content
✅ **Do This**: Refresh content every 3-6 months

❌ **Inconsistent NAP**: Different phone/address on different sites
✅ **Do This**: Exact same Name, Address, Phone everywhere

❌ **No Analytics**: Not tracking what's working
✅ **Do This**: Check metrics weekly, adjust strategy monthly
